# Database Entity Relationship Specification

## Primary Entities
- **User**: Student/Faculty record with registration number, college ID, branch, semester, and verification status.
- **AcademicSyllabus**: Hierarchical curriculum node mapping units, topics, and credit distributions.
- **PYQ**: Previous Year Question papers indexed by branch, semester, subject code, and examination year.
- **Note**: Peer-reviewed study material with file metadata, remarks score, and download analytics.
- **Notice**: Official university and college circulars with category tags, urgency levels, and attachment links.
- **MentorshipBooking**: Scheduled guidance sessions between junior students and verified senior mentors.

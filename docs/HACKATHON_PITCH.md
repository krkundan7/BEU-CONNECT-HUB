# Tejas India Hackathon 2026 - BEU Connect Hub Pitch

## Problem Statement
Engineering students under Bihar Engineering University lack a centralized, verified ecosystem for updated curriculum notes, authentic PYQ solutions, official alerts, and guided career pathways.

## Our Solution
A high-performance monorepo platform connecting 50,000+ BEU engineering students across 38 government colleges with verified syllabus tracking, AI mentor GPS, and peer learning tools.
